FLIGHT SCHOOL

60 SECOND BRIEF
01 — TAKE OFF

Your toy fighter launches already airborne. Hold SHIFT for boost, use Z / X or the mouse wheel for throttle, and keep the nose out of the rug.

02 — TURN FIGHT

Pitch and roll with WASD or the mouse. Bank hard, then add Q / E rudder to tighten the turn. The yaw rate is lower than the roll rate — use both.

03 — SHOOT

Put the yellow reticle on a bandit and hold SPACE or LEFT MOUSE. Rockets fly with R or RIGHT MOUSE. Rockets track whatever you have locked in your sights; when you hear the lock warning, pop F flares and break. Chain kills without dying: three in a row refills rapid fire, five adds armour, seven repairs your hull, and ten reloads your rockets.

04 — SURVIVE

Health regenerates only when you respawn. Watch the smoke trail and break off when your wings are red. Fly through windows, under the dining table, and around the garden to lose a tail. Shoot down balloons and fly through their drops — the cyan shield soaks damage for ten seconds.

TOUCH PILOTS

Use the virtual stick with your left thumb. Throttle, fire, boost, rockets, flares and camera are on the right. Play in portrait or landscape. Landscape gives a wider view; headphones recommended.

SURVIVAL MODE

Survival gives you three lives and throws escalating waves at you. Every 30 seconds another hostile joins the party; from wave six they fly at Ace skill. Stay high, use the balloons and make the last life count.
